AI powered Earnings-Call Sentiment & Short-Term Stock Movement
MSc Capstone · Trinity College Dublin × EY · Completed Aug 2026
Code & dashboard

Problem

Earnings calls move markets, but the signal sits inside an hour of hedged executive language. Can the sentiment in a transcript predict which way a stock moves in the days after the call — and can you prove the result isn't noise?

Approach

Built and deployed an interactive AI 3-day post-earnings directions for S&P 500 companies by scoring 10,599 S&P 500 transcripts (2021–2026) with VADER, FinBERT and GPT-4o-mini, with a multi-agent reconciliation layer resolving model disagreement on 7.6% of segments, then trained 16 classifiers under a strict temporal split. My focus: the VADER and GPT-4o-mini scoring, the classifier suite, and co-building the dashboard.

Result

A 6-percentage-point lift over the majority baseline, holding across six robustness diagnostics including a label-shuffle placebo, a GPT training-cutoff contamination check and cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals. Delivered as a 5-module interactive Streamlit dashboard — the full pipeline ran on $27.75 of API spend.

Python GPT-4o-mini FinBERT VADER Streamlit Classification & Validation Financial Analytics
Greenwashing vs Greenhushing — ESG Disclosure Analytics
MSc ESG Analytics · Trinity College Dublin
MSc RESEARCH

Problem

Companies that overstate their sustainability record attract scrutiny; companies that quietly understate it do not. Both distort ESG ratings, and neither is visible from a headline score.

Approach

Applied LDA topic modelling and vocabulary-based NLP metrics to listed-company ESG disclosures, classifying firms along a disclosure spectrum rather than collapsing them into a single rating.

Result

A repeatable text-analytics method that separates the two behaviours and highlights where a company's reported ESG posture diverges from the substance of what it discloses.
